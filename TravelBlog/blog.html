  My Two-year Journey...

      You might be wondering what kind of a big decision I had to make that sent me traveling to Laoag and stay there for 2 years, right? Well, have you heard of a church called "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints" or "LDS Church"? nothing? no? not even just a whisper? O-kay, maybe it's a lot more common for people to call it the "Mormon Church". Does it ring a bell? Oh really? great then.


      Most people would call us Mormons... but to explain that.. is another story. So anyway, I being a member of that church had decided to serve as a Full-Time Misisonary at the age of 18. You know those two guys in white polo's with neckties and black name tags on their chest pocket who goes around everywhere talking to everybody? yeah, like one of those guys.


      I'd like to share to you some of my joyful and fulfiling moments in the best two years of my life as a missionary.

  First Day in Laoag

      First of all, Laoag City, it is one heck of hot place to be in, literally. While getting of the plane at the airport you can already see heat waves on the air around you. I was looking forward to get inside the airport because of the heat outside, only to find out that there was no airconditioning whatsoever! I wasn't sure if there really weren't but even though not as much outside it was still pretty hot, and I could only see ceiling fans all over the place.


      After I got my luggage I went out and looked for those that were supposed to be waiting for my, and many more, arrival. We all got into a jeepney with our luggage on another one and went to the Mission Home. Its a great place for us to rest a bit from our flight, we got to eat some food as well. Afterwards, we were called into a room where we were oriented with our purpose there, do's and dont's, stuff like that... and we also got to meet our first companions.


      The first day was pretty tiring already I had no idea what was coming up in the near future from that day onwards. After a day or two we all set out to our respective areas, seeing each other only from time to time when plans coincide.

  Sta. Teresita, Cagayan...

      My very first area was in Cagayan, yeah all the way up there to te north. I had a bit of trouble for the first few weeks adjusting to the life here as someone from the urban areas like Manila, and as a missionary. Our apartment had 2 bedrooms and small living room with a kitchen all together. It was about 3 to 4 blocks from the highway and right in front of our house was the Barangay Jail, quite funny really XD. Later on after about 4.5 months we moved to another apartment by the highway this time around. It had a small porch out the front and goes all the way back so the size of the house is quite something measured lengthwise, it was a lot better than the old one so I was glad we moved.


      I had a great time here in Sta. Teresita, the people were very kind and nice, every monday we go shop for groceries at the local market and get to spend our time preparing for the week and sending emails to our families and loved ones. I got to experience a whole lot of stuff, getting lost in the middle of nowhere with literally no houses within sight, running through the cemetery at night while it was raining really hard, getting chased by about 7 dogs almost every other night going home, getting bit by one the dogs not really chasing us (I accidentally stepped on its tail XP), suddenly being told that what we were eating were dogs, and so much more.


      The weeks went by fast and before i knew it already 6 months had passed, and I literally knew each and every person we meet while walking down the road each day. Soon after, I was called to serve to a different area.

  Laoag Fifth, Ilocos Norte...

      Here I was, on a totally different area and culture, at a City. Far from my first area, the feelings and expectations were totally different. Over there I felt somewhat free and energized everyday, while here in the city it was kind of 'different' if you know what I mean. It was still fun though. Our apartment was part of a 3-division 2-storey building with ours being the 1st division and the last one being the landlords. We had a pretty small space but the 2nd floor somehow makes up for it anyway.


      I also spent my 2013 Christmas and 2014 New Year over here. Lots of parties and programs went on those weeks and everyone made noise on new year's eve... It was here that I found out you can walk through cities hahaha. While walking around one of our usual areas, we tried a new road we've never been through before. The road went on and on so we just kept walking, we met people along the way and also helped some on their work (carrying bags of corn back to their house). I knew we were pretty far off our usual area but then I saw an arch with a different barangay name on it so I thought "wow we travelled quite far, but... i never heard of this barangay name being part of our area". Looking through the maps we found out that it was already part of another City!


      We had to go back home or to our area but it would take about 3 hours on foot and we didn't have enough time for that so we decided to press on the same road, a few minutes passed and a black car stopped by our side, the window went down and the guy asked us if we'd like a ride noticing that we've been walking for a while. My companion and I looked at each other for a second and accepted the offer, luckily enough, he was headed to pick his wife up from work where he had to pass through our area to get there. While on the car we had time to chat and found out his wife had relatives that I most probably knew from my previous area assignment (what a small world). After getting off the car, we thanked him and went on our way.


      In only 3 months, I was transferred again to another area. It was funny since I only had to take a jeep and a tricy to get there.

  San Lorenzo, San Nicolas...

      "Wow" was the first thing that came to my mind when I arrived at the area. It was a mix of a rural and an urban feeling. In a town named San Nicolas was a barangay called San Lorenzo near the outskirts before reaching a different city. Our apartment had a big living room and and a kitchen in one on the first floor, with a lot of space since there weren't much furniture or anything around, and a bedroom with airconditioning and a bathroom as well. On the second floor, were 2 bedrooms and another bathroom. We had another big space like the living room and a terrace where I was able to hang my hammock I bought from my first area. It was a really big house.


      Our area was quite small comparing the my first two, it wasn't even a quarter of them. Our neighbors were one of the best parts of being there, they were very lively and very welcoming, specially the kids of the neighborhood. They were always together and playing around, just watch them and you'll forget all your problems in life hahaha. Life here was a bit bumpy though, quite a lot of ups and downs, but it was still great :). We did some community service from time to time, ate at our regular carinderia nearby, and ran through corn fields (I now know what it feels like running through them like in the movies :D). The livelihood of many came from, poultry farms, dragon fruit farms, garlic and onion farms, cows, and specially tobacco, tons and tons of tobacco.


      I expected for my time here to be a bit longer but alas it was not. I only stayed for about 15 weeks tops, and I had to go again.
