About Us

Wiggling Bat Games is a small mobile game development company operated by two passionate individuals. We specialize in creating fun and engaging games that are accessible to everyone. Our goal is to bring joy and entertainment to people's lives through our unique and creative game concepts.

We believe that games should be easy to play and hard to put down, and we strive to create experiences that captivate players for hours on end. Our target audience is anyone who enjoys playing mobile games and is looking for a new and exciting adventure.

Wiggling Bat Games is a General Partnership Company, which means that the business is owned and operated by two partners who share equal responsibility for its success. We chose this form of ownership because it allows us to maintain full control over the company and make decisions together. As partners, we have complementary skills and expertise that enable us to create high-quality games that are both fun and engaging.

Mission and Vision

Our MISSION is to develop innovative and entertaining mobile games that capture the essence of retro gaming while incorporating modern elements. We are committed to using best practices in game development to create games that are fun, challenging, and rewarding for our players. We aim to foster a culture of creativity, collaboration, and excellence in everything we do. With a focus on quality and user satisfaction, we aim to become a go-to source for retro-inspired mobile gaming experiences.

Our VISION is to be a leading mobile game development company that reimagines classic retro games for a modern audience. We strive to create games that evoke nostalgia while also incorporating cutting-edge technology and design. Our goal is to deliver high-quality games that provide engaging and immersive experiences for players of all ages and backgrounds.

Code of Conduct

Professionalism

Treat all team members, partners, and stakeholders with respect, honesty, and professionalism. Do not discriminate against or harass others based on their age, gender, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Avoid conflicts of interest or situations that may compromise your integrity or impartiality.

Game Content

Create games that are suitable for all ages and cultures, and that promote positive values and behaviors.

Avoid creating games that promote violence, hate, or intolerance, or that contain offensive or inappropriate content.

Respect the intellectual property rights of others, and do not infringe on their copyrights, trademarks, patents, or other intellectual property rights.

Privacy & Data Protection

Respect the privacy and data protection rights of our players and customers.

Comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations, and take appropriate measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access or use.


Do not disclose or share confidential or sensitive information without proper authorization.

Intellectual Property

Respect the intellectual property rights of others, and do not infringe on their copyrights, trademarks, patents, or other intellectual property rights.


Ensure that all content and assets used in our games are either original or properly licensed.

Environmental Responsibility

Promote sustainable practices and minimize our environmental impact.


Comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations, and take steps to reduce our energy consumption and waste.


Encourage and support sustainable practices among our team members, partners, and stakeholders.
